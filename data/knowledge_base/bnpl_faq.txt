BNPL CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

PAYMENT SCHEDULE

Customers can split eligible purchases into installment payments.
The number of installments and the payment schedule depend on
the purchase and customer eligibility.

LATE PAYMENT

If a customer misses a scheduled payment, the outstanding payment
remains due. Customers should review their payment schedule and
complete the outstanding payment as soon as possible.

REFUNDS

Refunds depend on the merchant's refund policy and the status
of the purchase. When a refund is approved, the corresponding
installment plan may be adjusted according to the applicable
refund process.

ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility for installment payments depends on several factors,
including the purchase, customer information, and internal
eligibility criteria.

PAYMENT METHODS

Customers can use supported payment methods to complete scheduled
installment payments. Available payment methods may vary depending
on the customer's account and region.
